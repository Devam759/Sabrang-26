Event Coordinators: Each event will have an assigned Event Coordinator. Participants/teams may reach out to them directly for event-specific queries, reporting times, and logistics. No Last-Minute Additions: No new participants will be allowed on the spot without prior approval and official confirmation. Organizers’ decision regarding scheduling, eligibility, and results shall be final and binding . No refunds post successful registration.
